# Privacy and safety — design requirements, not compliance certification

Review COPPA, FERPA where applicable, California SOPIPA, relevant California education/privacy laws, CCPA/CPRA as applicable, accessibility, automatic-renewal law, and vendor agreements with qualified counsel. Legal applicability and retention schedules TBD.

Use minimum necessary child data; no child email required. No microphone/precise location/child advertising identifiers in MVP. Separate family and institutional data; no automatic merge. Enforce server-side role+workspace+relationship+purpose+consent checks, deny by default. Encrypt, monitor, back up and test restores. No child-data sale or behavioral ad targeting. Marketing adult contact data separate from student learning data.

No production child processing before authority, consent, vendor, deletion, incident-response, access-control and accessibility requirements are reviewed and verified. No school student records solely because an individual educator pays for a subscription.
