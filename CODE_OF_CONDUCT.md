# Code of conduct — draft

Living Literacy aims to support a respectful, inclusive, and accessible community. Treat contributors, educators, families, students, and reviewers with dignity. Welcome good-faith questions and differing perspectives, give actionable criticism of work rather than attacking people, and respect others' time, privacy, and consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, disclosure of private information, and retaliation are unacceptable. Never post real student information, credentials, or someone else's personal data in project spaces. Avoid posting material that you lack permission to share.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project has **not yet appointed conduct contacts or approved a private reporting channel**. The owner must designate trained maintainers, a confidential reporting route, a conflict-of-interest alternative, and an enforcement process before opening public community spaces. Do not submit sensitive details in a public issue. This draft does not promise confidentiality, timelines, or outcomes that have not been established.

Once an approved channel is published, maintainers should handle reports fairly, limit access to the information, avoid retaliation, and document proportionate responses consistent with the approved process and applicable law.
