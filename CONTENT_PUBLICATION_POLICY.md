# Content publication policy

- Draft -> expert review -> approved -> published -> suspended/retired.
- `approved_for_prototype` means synthetic-data internal use only; not child-facing publication.
- Production content requires qualified reviewer identity, decision ID, approval scope, source verification (for nonfiction), accessibility review and version pinning.
- Source passage changes invalidate dependent Story Vibes scripts/audio until reviewed.
- No AI self-approval; no invented quotes or unsupported nonfiction facts.
- No automatic conversion of practice completion into mastery.
- Commercial photo rights must be verified independently; no implied celebrity endorsement.
