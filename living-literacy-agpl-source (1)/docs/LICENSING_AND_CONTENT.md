# Licensing and content provenance decisions

## Status: software license and proposed release scope selected

The project owner selected the **GNU Affero General Public License version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only)** for the software, and approved the current Living Literacy name, included sample lesson and project documents for the proposed public release. See the root `LICENSE` for the license text. The local repository has not yet been published.

This approval does not by itself establish a separate license for educational content, documents, other assets or trademarks, nor does it establish rights held by any third party. Verify authorship, sources, permission and notices as applicable. A simulated content-review decision is not educational or scientific approval for use with real learners.

Still to resolve: contributor terms and inbound rights; private security and conduct reporting contacts; and the provenance and licensing of any additional curriculum, media, third-party material, or future contributions. Record subsequent approvals and scopes without inventing review results. Do not import proprietary assessment questions, scoring algorithms, designs, or unlicensed media.

## Minimum content provenance record

For each proposed release version of a lesson, passage, assessment form, worksheet, audio item or other asset, record:

- Stable ID, version, title, content type, author/rights holder, creation date, and version history.
- Source and citations, access date and nonfiction fact-check/verification date where applicable; clearly label original fiction and reviewed retellings. Record quotations accurately and do not invent source claims.
- Rights evidence and permitted uses, license/version or explicit permission, attribution and notice requirements, redistribution or modification restrictions, and expiration if applicable. Identify separately licensed embedded assets.
- Accessible formats and review status; the independent qualified reviewer, decision identifier, date, scope, and approval level. A simulated identity or `approved_for_prototype` is not student-facing approval.
- For derived scripts or audio: source passage and version, mode (verbatim or reviewed retelling), script and asset versions, rights, narrator permissions, integrity digest and status. Withdraw or re-review dependent assets when the source changes.
- Publication, suspension and retirement history, correction process, and version-pinned references in learning records. Do not erase historical evidence merely to revise a published asset.

Review sources before publication; do not import proprietary assessment questions, scoring algorithms, content, designs, or unlicensed media. No unsupported diagnostic, Lexile, grade-level, efficacy, or mastery claims are allowed.
