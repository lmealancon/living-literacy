# Story Vibes requirements

Pipeline: approved source version → generation candidate → factual/fidelity and safety review → qualified human approval → TTS generation → audio QA → approved asset → authorized playback.

Four styles: The Tea; Bro, Listen; Movie Mode; Reading Coach. Read Exactly must match approved source; Story Vibes is labeled expressive retelling and cannot invent nonfiction facts/quotes. No peer/celebrity impersonation or voice cloning without rights. Prefer preapproved/cached audio in first release. Text and accessibility fallback required.

Store passage version, script version, asset version, checksum, approvals, and invalidation status. Record narration exposure when it affects independence of decoding evidence.
