# Mandatory release gates

1. Auth and tenant isolation verified.
2. Applicable child authority/consent and privacy notices approved.
3. Data minimization, retention/deletion, vendor review and incident response approved.
4. Approved instructional content and licensed assets.
5. No unsupported diagnostic/mastery/efficacy claims.
6. Story Vibes fidelity, safety and assistance provenance verified if enabled.
7. Accessibility checks performed and critical defects resolved.
8. Backup restore, monitoring, rollback and support readiness verified.
9. Billing and consumer terms verified if charging.
10. All critical test results and human approvals recorded.

Prototype using synthetic data is not authorization to launch with real children.
