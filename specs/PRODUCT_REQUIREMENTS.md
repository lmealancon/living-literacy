# Product requirements (draft)

## Audience
Primary: families supporting readers in grades 3–8; wider K–8 architecture. Secondary: individual educators. Schools/districts gated future expansion.

## MVP: Teach & Practice
Adult accounts; authorized child profiles (only after consent/privacy implementation); reviewed lesson library; printables; six-stage lesson player; response and assistance provenance; accurate activity history; transparent next-practice suggestions; optional preapproved Story Vibes; accessible mobile-first UI; useful free access.

## Not in MVP
Unrestricted child AI chat, automatic mastery, unvalidated comprehensive K–8 diagnostic, Lexile/grade equivalents, disability diagnosis, high-stakes speech recognition, district analytics, efficacy claims.

## Pedagogy
Greatest demonstrated need drives priority; word recognition and language comprehension developed concurrently. Dependency relationships do not blanket-lock the other strand.

## Plans
Free $0; Family Plus $9.99 monthly; Solo Educator $19.99 monthly. School licenses future. Educator student-roster rights require separate institutional authorization.

## Story Vibes
Styles: The Tea; Bro, Listen; Movie Mode; Reading Coach. Modes: Read Exactly (verbatim) and Story Vibes (labeled reviewed retelling). Preapproved assets, accessible captions/transcripts, reduced stimulation. No fabricated nonfiction claims or unauthorized voice impersonation.
