# Assessment and evidence restrictions

Evidence chain: observation → quality review → scoring interpretation → eligibility → decision evaluation → authorized action → audit/correction.

Keep separate: activity completed; skill practiced; independently demonstrated; retained/transferred; broader reading achievement improved. Record task conditions, version, assistance and provenance.

States proposed: not_assessed, developing, independently_demonstrated, retained, needs_review. Automated assignments of consequential mastery states DISABLED; thresholds unset until validation.

A 30–35-item K–8 screener is only a concept; do not call it a validated comprehensive diagnostic. Do not derive Lexile, grade equivalent, disability diagnosis or efficacy claims from it.
