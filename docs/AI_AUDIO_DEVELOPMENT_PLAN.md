# AI speaking, listening and narration development plan

Status: engineering plan, not an implemented or approved child-facing feature. Use fictional data only. The supplied package contains no complete approved lesson inventory, approved audio recordings, narration scripts, verified human reviewers, child identity provider, consent framework, or validated speech-recognition system. `AGENTS.md`, `specs/STORY_VIBES.md`, `specs/PRIVACY_SECURITY.md`, `specs/RELEASE_GATES.md` and `docs/DECISION_REGISTER.md` govern this work. D-014 defers a live child AI tutor and automatic speech recognition; microphone use is excluded from the MVP. An automated response must not be represented as a reviewed lesson, independent reading evidence, or a diagnosis.

## Deliverable sequence

1. **Approved narration and listening, without a microphone.** Start with a version-pinned source passage and script; attach a licensed, human-reviewed, accessible prerecorded asset with transcript/captions and any source, author, reviewer, rights and approval records. A synthetic demonstration may use simulated fixtures only and must be labeled accordingly; simulated approvals are not human approval. The server must check identity, role, workspace, relationship, purpose, consent, content status, version, and digest before use, deny by default, and record audio exposure as assistance in the same transaction as the playback authorization. Withdrawals prevent subsequent delivery or exercise writes while authorized history follows approved retention rules. Provide pause, resume, playback speed, captions, transcript and a non-audio text option.
2. **Reviewed listening activities.** Deliver separately reviewed, versioned listening prompts and answer formats. Record the audio exposure and response conditions; listening support cannot establish independent decoding. Do not invent passage facts, answers, thresholds, grade-equivalent scores, diagnoses or skill definitions. No child-facing content is enabled until the appropriate human review and release gates are met.
3. **Constrained adult synthetic voice experiment only after an explicit task and provider decision.** Select a vendor or local provider only after data-flow and cost review. Use fixed, approved scripts and synthetic data; no uncontrolled prompts or open-ended child chat. Do not collect real audio or use an unapproved voice identity. Define limits, error/fallback behavior and deletion before processing anything; never expose provider credentials to the browser. This phase does not override D-014 or the MVP microphone restriction for children.
4. **Optional future child speaking and listening.** This is a later product decision, not a feature enabled by a configuration flag. First resolve D-014, actual authority/consent, age-appropriate notice, vendor processing and retention, school and family tenancy, offline/nonvoice alternatives, child-safety behavior, provider and accessibility reviews, data minimization, incident response, content validity, and independent security and legal review. Speech recognition should not penalize accent, dialect, disability or English-learner status; uncertain outputs need human review. The application cannot claim validated speech scoring or diagnosis without evidence and explicit approval.

## Prototype interfaces and test obligations

- Prefer vendor-neutral adapters that are explicitly configured, versioned and removable. Unavailable provider, missing permission, stale approval, withdrawal, malformed input, timeouts and provider errors must fail closed and leave an accessible text fallback. Avoid third-party calls for a read-only local/offline experience.
- Require content pinning, server-side relationship and purpose checks, safe asset loading and checksum verification. Audio recording or transcription requires a separate explicitly authorized scope and review; playback consent is not microphone consent. Never ask an LLM to approve its own source, invent factual citations, or impersonate a celebrity.
- Test against cross-family access, withdrawn content/consent, tampered files, mismatched script/source, Read Exactly equality, retelling labels, caption absence, provider failure and exposure logging. Test assisted audio paths cannot become independent decoding evidence. Test browser keyboard controls and reduced motion, and verify nothing leaves the device when the offline path is selected. Include synthetic-only test fixtures, and report actual results.
- Durable storage, access-controlled backups, data deletion, audit, and lifecycle/version handling must be reviewed and tested before real records are permitted. The existing free Render staging database can reset on sleep or restart, and no real-user security or uptime claim is supported.

## Decisions and inputs still needed

- Independently reviewed lesson/passage records and licensed narration scripts and audio, with reviewers and proof of rights. The fictional `map` sample is a software fixture; its simulated approval does not make it student-ready.
- Approved candidate skill definitions and assessment materials; the 91-count manifest does not supply the definitions.
- An explicit decision on microphone/speech use and D-014, only after privacy and authority controls can be reviewed. Provider, voice rights, cost limits, operation/storage policies and delivery hosting remain undecided.
- Access to the live hosting and source-control workflows to deploy and verify changes; local code changes do not automatically appear in the online site.
